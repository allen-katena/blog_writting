# Katena Blog: Writing Goal

## Brief

Every article on the Katena blog has one job: make the right people want to be part of what Katena is building. Those people are investors, industry partners and sponsors, and the engineers we want to hire. Readers should leave each piece seeing three things: humanoid robotics is a real and growing opportunity, Katena understands it better than most, and Katena is a team worth backing, partnering with or joining. Articles should be engaging and easy to read without a technical background, and still credible enough to hold up when an expert reads them.

## Three things every article must show

1. **The opportunity is real.** Show where the industry is heading and why it matters now: the market, the momentum, and the problems still waiting to be solved. Investors should see upside, partners should see where they fit, and engineers should see work worth doing.
2. **Katena knows what it's talking about.** Bring a clear point of view backed by solid facts and first-hand experience that others don't have. Every article should leave the reader thinking, "these people understand this space from the inside."
3. **Katena is where it's happening.** Connect the topic to what Katena is building and doing, naturally and without turning the article into an ad. Readers should finish wanting to follow, talk to, sponsor, invest in or work at Katena.
